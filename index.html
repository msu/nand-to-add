NAND Game

Build every gate from NAND. Finish with an 8-bit adder.

Toolbox

Click a gate to add it to the board.

    Levels

      Drag from a port to another port to make a wire. Click an input pin to toggle it. Click a gate or wire, then press Delete to remove it. Right click also removes.
